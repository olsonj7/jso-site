Linkside Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Linkside is a product of JSO Solutions LLC (“JSO Solutions,” “Linkside,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Linkside mobile application, website (getlinkside.com and app.getlinkside.com), mobile golf simulator rentals, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: phone number, name, email address, and a Google or Apple sign-in identifier if you use those options (Apple may provide a private relay address). Passwords, if used, are stored hashed.
  • Profile: optional profile photo, favorite courses, and handicap and scoring information you enter.
  • Golf Activity: tee times, golf trips, outings, tournaments, RSVPs, scores and scorecards, polls, and the chat messages, notes, and photos you post.
  • Contact Information You Provide: names and phone numbers (and sometimes emails) of golf buddies or invitees that you choose to add or invite, and the groups and saved golfers you create.
  • Contacts: only with your permission, phone numbers from your address book are sent to our servers to show which friends already use Linkside. This check is done in real time and the address-book data is not kept.
  • Location: only when you search for nearby golf courses or tee times or request weather. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Camera, Photos, and Microphone: used only when you scan a scorecard, record a swing for AI analysis, or upload a photo. For swing analysis we send still frames and body-position data, not the full video.
  • Simulator Rental Bookings: name, email, phone, event and home address, event details, the rental agreement you sign (including your typed name, the time, and your IP address), and your damage-charge authorization. Payments are handled by Stripe; we keep the Stripe customer and payment-method references needed to charge your balance, not your card number.
  • Payments: entry fees, trip deposits, and simulator deposits and balances are processed by Stripe, including Apple Pay and Google Pay.
  • Technical Information: a push-notification token, device and app version, IP address, logs, and crash reports.
  • Communications Data: message delivery status, opt-in and opt-out records, HELP/STOP requests, and support communications.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • create and secure your account, verify your phone number, and send one-time passcodes;
  • run the features you use, including tee times, invites and RSVPs, trips, tournaments, chat, scorecards, handicap and stats, and simulator bookings;
  • send invitations, reminders, receipts, booking and payment emails, and notifications you have not turned off;
  • provide AI features you choose to use (swing check, scorecard scanning, round recaps, invite and weather text);
  • take payments, prevent fraud and abuse, keep the Services secure, fix problems, and provide customer support; and
  • comply with legal obligations and carrier/platform messaging requirements.

3. Text Messaging and Mobile Information

By signing up for Linkside or otherwise opting in, you agree to receive text messages related to your account and use of the Services, including authentication codes, golf round invitations, RSVP updates, and other service-related communications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with vendors, platform providers, or subcontractors that help us deliver our messaging services.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information with service providers that help us run the Services, and only what they need:

  • Supabase and Railway: database, file storage, and hosting.
  • Twilio: text messages and phone verification.
  • Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications.
  • Google and Apple: if you choose Sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Stripe: payment processing, including Stripe Connect for tournament and trip payments.
  • Resend: transactional email.
  • Anthropic: AI features. Content you submit for them (swing frames, scorecard photos, round or weather details) is sent through our servers for that request.
  • Google Maps Platform, Open-Meteo, and GolfCourseAPI: course search, distance, weather, and course data (this can include a search term, ZIP code, address, or coordinates).
  • PostHog and Sentry: product analytics on our website (stored in your browser, with no advertising cookies) and crash reports.

Other people in your groups, trips, tee times, and tournaments can see what you share with them (for example your name, RSVP, and chat messages). We may also disclose information in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, or where necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect our rights, or prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues.

5. User-Provided Contact Information

If you provide another person’s phone number or contact details through Linkside, you represent that you have the right to do so and that you have permission to send that person a golf round invitation or related communication through the Services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations (for example tax and accounting records for payments and bookings), resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account), at getlinkside.com/delete-account, or by emailing support@getlinkside.com. Deleting your account deactivates it and removes you from invites; we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days of your request, except records we are required or permitted to keep.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and secure storage of sign-in tokens. Card details are handled by Stripe. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

  • You may opt out of text messaging by replying STOP, and turn off push notification categories in the app or your device settings.
  • You may withdraw Contacts, Location, Camera, or Photos permission at any time in your device settings (some features will then stop working).
  • You may contact us to access, correct, or delete your information. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights, and we will honor valid requests.
  • You may decline to provide certain information, but some features may not function properly.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Services are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the Effective Date above and post the revised version on this page. The current version is also available at getlinkside.com/privacy-policy.
